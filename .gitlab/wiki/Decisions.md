# Decisions

> 🦊 **kri Wiki** · [Home](Home) · [Repo Index](Repo-Index) · [Operating Principles](Operating-Principles) · [Glossary](Glossary)


Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) live in `docs/decisions/` across the repos.

## Convention

- Numbered (`0001-...`), one decision per file, plus `TEMPLATE.md`
- **Accepted ADRs are immutable** — supersede with a new ADR, never edit
- Changes land via MR only, never committed directly
